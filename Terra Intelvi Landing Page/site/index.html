A home in the green, made for slowing down

Terra Intelvi is our family apartment in the hills of Valle d'Intelvi, halfway between Lake Como and Lake Lugano. Mornings here start with birdsong and mountain air. Evenings end on the sofa after a day of lakes, trails and long lunches.

It's not a hotel. It's a real home, cared for and quietly comfortable, with everything a family of four needs to settle in for a week or more.
